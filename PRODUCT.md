# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A broad creative audience building interactive branching stories:
- writers and authors shaping prose and structure;
- game narrative designers wiring dialogue, quests, variables and conditions;
- educators and students building interactive lessons and choose-your-path scenarios;
- hobbyists experimenting for fun.

They work in long, focused sessions on the web or the desktop app. They move between writing text inside nodes, wiring logic across a canvas, and play-testing the result. Many aren't programmers.

## Product Purpose
NodeTale is a visual, node-based editor for interactive branching narratives. It turns a story's structure (choices, conditions, jumps, variables, media) into something you can see, edit and play. Success means an author can hold a large branching story in their head through the tool: see where paths split and rejoin, spot dead ends and unused logic, and play any path instantly.

## Brand Personality
A blend, weighted by context:
- **focused and crafted** while writing (the tool stays quiet; the story is the loudest thing on screen);
- **precise** when wiring logic and inspecting structure (dense, exact, keyboard-friendly);
- **atmospheric** where the story itself shows through (covers, play mode);
- **playful** in how it invites experimenting (first run, empty states, exploring branches).

Voice is plain and encouraging; it never talks down to non-programmers.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: glassy or gradient cards, hero stat blocks, purple-blue "AI" gradients, decorative blur. The current dashboard leans this way and is being replaced.
- Identical icon-heading-text card grids and marketing-style section scaffolding inside the app.

## Design Principles
1. **The story is the interface.** Show the story's own shape (its graph, its branches, its timeline) instead of generic chrome. Recognizing a project by its structure beats reading a label.
2. **Quiet by default, dense on demand.** Writing surfaces stay calm; structure and logic views can be information-rich when the author asks for them.
3. **Every view leads back to the canvas.** Overviews exist to find and fix things. Clicking anything should take you to it.
4. **Make problems visible, not scary.** Dead ends, unreachable nodes and unused variables surface as calm, specific signals, not error walls.
5. **Familiar affordances, crafted details.** Standard controls and navigation, done with care, with no invented widgets for flavour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 for large text and UI graphics, full keyboard operation, and visible focus.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: animations get an instant or crossfade alternative.
- Never rely on colour alone. Branch and path colours in the story views are paired with labels, shapes or patterns, since authors may be colour-blind.
